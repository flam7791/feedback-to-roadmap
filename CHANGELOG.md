# Changelog

## v0.1.0 - 2026-10-23

First public version: the MVP and its product and team pack.

- Feedback from in-app comments, survey answers, tickets and interview notes in one CSV, stopped on malformed rows (US-01); personal data redacted at intake (US-02); instruction-like text flagged and excluded (US-03)
- Coding with a codebook the product owner owns, manual codes overriding (US-04); uncoded queue with new-theme candidates (US-05); codebook measured on dev and held-out gold sets (US-16)
- Evidence in people, not rows: channels, segments, severity, lapsed reporters, tier and estimated reach (US-06); exact verbatim quotes (US-07); silent segments (US-08); spikes routed to support (US-09)
- Proposed reach and confidence changes, each with its rule and numbers (US-10); opinion-only items and gaps (US-11); ranking before and after with the capacity cut line and pins (US-12)
- Only a product owner decides, with notes for rejections and pins (US-13); apply writes a new roadmap version, never the source, and refuses on drift (US-14); evidence pack in Markdown, HTML and JSON, decision record and people to tell (US-15); usage events in the ai-adoption-tracker format (US-17)
- Product pack (discovery, PRD, prioritisation, roadmap, metrics and pilot, decision log, worked example) and team pack (brief, RACI, backlog, iteration plan, working agreement); CI enforces that every Done story has a test
